AayuCare (आयु-Care)

System Functional Requirements Specification (SRS) • Elderly Care & Medication Platform

1. Executive Project Mission & Core Problem Statement

Slide 13 Official Mandate: Develop a smart, interconnected healthcare management platform helping elderly individuals manage daily medications, medical appointments, and healthcare routines while keeping family members and caretakers informed through secure passcode-based access and proactive email reminders, ensuring elders remain at the center of their own healthcare decisions.

AayuCare bridges the critical human gap between elderly individuals, their busy caretakers, and clinical prescriptions. Unlike conventional reminder apps that demand manual smartphone interactions, AayuCare acts as a zero-cognitive-burden ambient companion utilizing real visual pill identification, natural voice confirmation, anti-overdose locks, and proactive family escalation.

2. System Actors & Persona Roles

Persona Responsibility / Access Primary Touchpoint
Elderly Patient (Babuji/Mataji) Consumer of care. Views visual pills, hears audio announcements, marks consumed via voice or 1-tap. Passive Ambient Tablet / Bedside Phone (Zero complex menus, massive accessible buttons).
Primary Caregiver (Daughter/Son/Nurse) Administrator. Uploads prescriptions, binds pill photos, edits schedules, handles escalations. Caretaker Cockpit (Passcode-linked, schedule manager, adherence charts).
Family Observer (Relative) Observer. Views weekly adherence, appointment dates, and emergency alerts. Mobile Web Dashboard (Read-only status sync via CarePasscode).

3. Detailed Functional Modules Specification

Module 1: Shared Ecosystem & CarePasscode Access (FR-1)

  • FR-1.1 (Elder Profile Creation): Admin or elder creates profile with Name, Age, Blood Group, Primary Physician, and Emergency Phone.
  • FR-1.2 (Unique CarePasscode): System generates a 6-character hashed access key (e.g. CARE-8492).
  • FR-1.3 (Zero-Friction Caretaker Pairing): Family members link to the elder profile simply by supplying the CarePasscode, eliminating multi-account onboarding friction.
  • FR-1.4 (Role-Based Access Control): Separation between PRIMARY_CAREGIVER (full write permissions) and FAMILY_OBSERVER (read-only audit access).

Module 2: Smart Prescription Ingestion & Dietary Guidance (FR-2)

  • FR-2.1 (Vision AI Prescription Extraction): Caretaker uploads a photo of the clinical prescription. Adaption AI / Multimodal Vision extracts:
    • Medication names, dosage metrics (e.g., 500mg, 1 tablet), and timing frequency.
    • Dietary Guidelines & Warnings: Specific clinical rules (e.g., "Take post-meal", "Avoid grapefruit or high-potassium foods").
  • FR-2.2 (Visual Pill Photo Binding): Caretaker snaps an actual photo of the physical tablet strip/capsule, attaching it to the medication schedule for elder visual recognition.
  • FR-2.3 (Criticality Tagging):
    • 🔴 High-Risk Meds: Blood pressure, diabetes, blood thinners, cardiac drugs (Triggers immediate 45-min proactive escalation on miss).
    • 🟢 Routine Meds: Multivitamins, calcium, antacids (Silent adherence log).

Module 3: Ambient Visual Display & Voice-First Interaction (FR-3)

  • FR-3.1 (Scheduled Ambient Takeover): At the exact scheduled time, the device screen awakens with a full-screen display of the Real Tablet Photo, meal timing, and large bold instructions.
  • FR-3.2 (Text-to-Speech Audio Announcement): The device synthesizes polite bilingual/Hindi voice audio: "Namaste Babuji, aapki subah ki BP wali goli ka time ho gaya hai. Kripya peeli wali goli lijiye."
  • FR-3.3 (Dual Interaction Confirmation):
    • Voice Input: Natural speech recognition listening for confirmation phrases ("Kha li", "Maine le li", "Done").
    • Giant Tap Redundancy: An unmissable 80px high green button ([ ✔️ MAINE LE LI ]) for environments with background fan/TV noise.

Module 4: Adherence Tracking & Anti-Overdose Shield (FR-4)

  • FR-4.1 (State Tracking): Logs medication events under strict states: SCHEDULED, TAKEN, MISSED, SKIPPED with exact millisecond timestamps and confirmation method.
  • FR-4.2 (Anti-Double-Dosing Lockout Shield): Once marked taken, that slot locks into an active Green Shield for 6 hours: "Babuji, aapne 9:15 AM par ye dose le li hai. Agli dose raat 8:00 PM ko hai." Prevents dementia-related fatal repeat consumption.
  • FR-4.3 (Clinical Catch-Up Window): If an elder attempts to consume a dose >3 hours late, the AI advises: "Dawai ka samay nikal chuka hai. Kripya double dose na lein, agli dose nirdharit samay par lein."

Module 5: Proactive Family Escalation & Email Engine (FR-5)

  • FR-5.1 (Automated Escalation Protocol):
    • T + 0 min: Gentle elder chime and audio announcement.
    • T + 30 min: Secondary gentle nudge to elder.
    • T + 45 min: Critical Escalation: Automated email alert dispatched to Primary Caretaker with patient name, overdue medication, and direct call trigger.
  • FR-5.2 (Appointment Alert Dispatcher): Automated email reminders dispatched 24 hours and 2 hours prior to scheduled doctor visits.

Module 6: Doctor Appointments & Clinical Summary Briefcase (FR-6)

  • FR-6.1 (Appointment Scheduler): Doctor name, medical specialty (Cardiology, Orthopedics), clinic address, appointment date/time, and pre-visit consultation notes.
  • FR-6.2 (1-Click Clinical Adherence Passport): Single-button generation of a 30-day compliance summary for doctors (Adherence rate percentage, missed dates list, active drug schedule).

Module 7: Caretaker Cockpit & Live Telemetry (FR-7)

  • FR-7.1 (Live Adherence Metrics): Real-time percentage scorecard (e.g. 94% weekly adherence score).
  • FR-7.2 (Real-Time Activity Feed): Audit log showing every elder interaction (e.g., "Babuji confirmed Metformin via voice at 9:02 AM").
  • FR-7.3 (Single-Click Emergency SOS): One-touch distress beacon broadcasting to all care-circle members.
